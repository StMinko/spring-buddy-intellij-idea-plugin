Reports cascade = CascadeType.ALL on @ManyToOne or @ManyToMany associations.

The owning side of a many-to-one relationship should not cascade lifecycle operations to the parent — deleting a child can unintentionally delete shared parents. Restrict cascading to the operations actually needed, or move the cascade to the inverse @OneToMany side.

A @ManyToOne whose @JoinColumn declares unique = true is not reported: the target row belongs to exactly one owner, so the association is a one-to-one in disguise and cascading cannot reach a shared parent.
