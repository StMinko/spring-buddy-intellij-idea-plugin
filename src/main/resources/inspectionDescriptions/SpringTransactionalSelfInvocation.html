Self-invocation bypasses @Transactional proxy

Reports same-bean calls to @Transactional methods. Spring proxy advice is applied when callers go through the proxy, not when one method invokes another on this. Move the call to another bean or invoke through a proxy-aware boundary.

Calls are not reported when the callee does not change transaction semantics, including a readOnly-only override on a write transaction. Calls from a read-only caller to a writable callee remain reported.
