Checked exception may not trigger rollback

Reports @Transactional methods that declare checked exceptions without rollbackFor or rollbackOn. By default Spring rolls back on unchecked exceptions only. Add rollbackFor/rollbackOn or convert the failure to an unchecked exception if rollback is required.

For methods with a body, the inspection reports only checked exceptions that can escape the body. Effective read-only transactions without detected write operations are exempt. Exceptions from calls that cannot be resolved do not contribute to the analysis; methods without a body retain declaration-based reporting.
