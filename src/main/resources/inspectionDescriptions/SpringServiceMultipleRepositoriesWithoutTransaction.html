Service method uses multiple repositories without @Transactional

This inspection reports service methods that call multiple repositories without an explicit @Transactional boundary. If one write succeeds and a later operation fails, data can be partially committed. Add @Transactional to the service method or refactor the operations into a transactional service boundary.

Repository writes inside callbacks passed to Spring's TransactionTemplate or TransactionOperations, and private helpers whose callers are transactionally covered or absent, are not reported.
