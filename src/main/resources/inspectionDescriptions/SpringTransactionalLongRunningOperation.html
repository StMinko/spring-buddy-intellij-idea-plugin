Long-running work inside @Transactional

Reports transactional methods that appear to perform slow external work such as network/client calls or Thread.sleep. Holding a database transaction while waiting on external I/O can exhaust the connection pool. Use a transaction sandwich: load, call external service outside a transaction, then save.

Calls are identified by client-like receiver types, such as *Client, RestTemplate, RestOperations, or WebClient. Generic method names such as get and execute do not count on their own.
