Service method swallows broad exception

This inspection reports service methods that catch broad exceptions such as Exception and do not rethrow them. Swallowing errors hides failures from callers and can leave the system in an inconsistent state. Rethrow the exception, convert it to a meaningful domain exception, or move error handling to a boundary where the failure can be handled explicitly.

A broad catch that calls a logger's info, warn, warning, error, severe, fatal, or log method is not reported.
