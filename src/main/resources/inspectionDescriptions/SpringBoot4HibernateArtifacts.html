Reports build dependencies whose coordinates target Hibernate 6 or Jackson 2 while Spring Boot 4 runs Hibernate 7 and Jackson 3: com.fasterxml.jackson.datatype:jackson-datatype-hibernate5/hibernate6 and io.hypersistence:hypersistence-utils-hibernate-63.

Spring Boot 4 upgrades to Hibernate 7 and Jackson 3. The Hibernate Jackson module moved to tools.jackson.datatype:jackson-datatype-hibernate7 and the hypersistence-utils artifact line follows the Hibernate major version (hypersistence-utils-hibernate-71).

Quick fix renames the artifacts and the groupId in Maven and Gradle build files.
